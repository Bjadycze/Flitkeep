## English

### Název
```
Flitkeep
```

### Krátký popis (78 znaků)
```
Catch the thought behind a screenshot before it's gone. No badges, no pressure.
```

### Dlouhý popis
```
You take a screenshot because something caught your attention. An hour later it
sits in your gallery among forty others and you have no idea why it's there.

Flitkeep notices that screenshot the moment it appears and gives you a few seconds
for one sentence: what it was for. You write it straight from the notification,
without even unlocking the phone. That's the whole thing.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

The app reads the text in the image by itself, so you can find things by what was
written on them. It recognises dates, amounts, phone numbers and order codes. It
sorts items into categories, and you can correct those or add your own.

Once a day, at a time you choose, one quiet reminder arrives. Three things at most.
When there's nothing worth reminding you about, nothing arrives.

WHY IT DOESN'T NAG

This app is designed for people with ADHD, and that mostly means one thing: it must
never turn into a pile of unfinished tasks that makes you feel worse every time you
open it.

• One notification a day at most. Not one per item.
• Three items per reminder at most.
• Nothing to show means nothing is sent. No "you're all caught up".
• An item is brought back three times at most, then moves to a quiet archive. It is
  never deleted and stays findable.
• No badges, no streaks, no unread counts, no red dots. They aren't switched off —
  they're prohibited by design.

The only reward fires when you tick something off. It lasts half a second and leaves
no record. It rewards what you do, never the state you're in.

PRIVACY

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no
analytics, no advertising. Text recognition runs on the device.

The only time the app uses the internet is when you share a link into it — then it
fetches that page's title and preview so you'll recognise it a week later.

The source code is public: github.com/Bjadycze/Flitkeep

WHAT IT DOES

• Notices a screenshot and offers a note within three seconds
• Accepts shared content from any app
• Reads text from images on the device
• Recognises dates, amounts, phone numbers, order codes
• Automatic and custom categories
• Swipe a card to settle it
• Search across notes and recognised text, archive included
• Six colour palettes, light and dark
• Czech and English

A NOTE ABOUT PHONE SETTINGS

To notice screenshots after a reboot or overnight, the app needs a battery
optimisation exemption and auto-launch enabled. It walks you through this on first
run. On Honor, Huawei and Xiaomi phones this is required — without it the system
shuts the app down in the background.
```

4. Nastavení s paletami

dělat oba listingy.
